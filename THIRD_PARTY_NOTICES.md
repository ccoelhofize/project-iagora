# Third-party notices

**Status:** Active
**Owner:** Maintainers
**Last reviewed:** 2026-07-29

The first vertical slice has no third-party runtime package dependency. It uses
the Python standard library.

Third-party civic evidence and data are not software dependencies and are not
covered by the root EUPL grant. Their source-specific rights, attribution, and
redistribution state are recorded in the pilot source inventory and the nearest
`data/` manifest.

This inventory must be updated before adding or distributing a dependency or
third-party asset.

The archived Olivier Bianchi 2020 campaign page is not redistributed in this
repository. Only governance metadata, its fingerprint, a precise locator, and a
short citation are retained. The archived legal notice reserves reproduction
rights; qualified review or permission is required before broader reuse.

The municipal administrative PDFs reviewed for the pilot are also not
redistributed. The repository retains source URLs, acquisition metadata,
fingerprints, precise paraphrased fragments, and documented limits. This avoids
redistributing material before qualified rights review and avoids retaining
unnecessary personal contact details present in at least one communication.
