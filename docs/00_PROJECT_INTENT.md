# Project Intent

**Status:** Accepted  
**Owner:** Maintainers  
**Accepted:** 2026-07-28  
**Deciders:** Project maintainer  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-07-28

## Purpose

IAgora turns fragmented public information into knowledge that citizens can inspect rather than merely trust. It connects campaign commitments, competent public decisions, resources, delivery, observed outcomes, and causally defensible impacts while preserving the evidence and uncertainty behind each conclusion.

## Intended outcome

A citizen, journalist, researcher, or public servant should be able to determine:

- what was stated, promised, decided, funded, delivered, and measured;
- which source is authoritative for each fact and which evidence supports or contradicts it;
- how an output was transformed from its source;
- what remains missing, disputed, restricted, or methodologically uncertain;
- whether an assessment concerns fulfillment, observed change, contribution, or causal impact.

## Initial validation

The first validation is the Clermont-Ferrand “Respire à la récré” pilot defined by [ADR-0001](adr/ADR-0001-project-vision-and-pilot-boundary.md). It uses an observation cut-off of 31 December 2025 and connects the original 2020 campaign commitment to later action and effects. Three school case studies may test traceability but cannot establish citywide impact without a suitable design.

## Boundaries

IAgora documents public action. It MUST NOT campaign, recommend political choices, rank political actors, monitor private citizens, infer political intent as fact, or present generated content as evidence. Public availability does not by itself authorize acquisition, retention, or republication.

## Success conditions

The foundation is credible only when:

- every material public assertion has precise evidence and end-to-end lineage;
- source authority remains fact-specific and separate from evidence quality;
- original evidence and claims remain inspectable through corrections and versions;
- conflicts, missing data, and uncertainty remain visible;
- fulfillment and impact methods are reproducible and resistant to political gaming;
- public explanations are accessible and machine-readable;
- privacy, security, retention, and licence gates are implemented before production use.

## Current reality

The repository contains accepted decisions and specifications alongside draft documentation. It contains no application, production pipeline, public dataset, deployed service, or evidence that the pilot has been completed.
