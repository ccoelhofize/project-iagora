# IAgora Manifesto

**Last reviewed:** 2026-07-28

## Transforming Data into Knowledge

Public information is not meaningful transparency when it is fragmented, inaccessible, or detached from its context. IAgora connects records without erasing their origin. It shows how a statement became a fact, calculation, assessment, or unresolved question.

## Transforming Knowledge into Empowerment

People should be able to inspect public action without specialist tools or blind trust. IAgora serves understanding: it helps citizens and institutions ask better questions, verify answers, and see the limits of available evidence.

## Our commitments

We put evidence before conclusions. We distinguish authority from supporting evidence. We preserve original material and record transformations. We keep credible conflicts visible. We explain methods, uncertainty, and corrections in plain language.

We use AI to assist discovery and explanation, never to manufacture facts or political judgment. We refuse surveillance of private citizens and unnecessary processing of personal data. We treat accessibility, security, legality, and reproducibility as parts of correctness.

We prefer small, inspectable systems over speculative scale. We design canonical concepts for reuse across territories while starting with one bounded, auditable pilot.

## What we will not become

IAgora is not a campaign tool, political ranking system, election predictor, reputation score, general social-media monitor, or authority that asks the public to accept unexplained conclusions.

## Standard of trust

Trust should arise from inspectability. Every important result should reveal its definition, source, evidence, transformation, scope, time, uncertainty, rights, and governing decision.
